About MatrixIntervals

Practice musical intervals with purpose.

MatrixIntervals is a music theory practice game designed to help musicians become faster and more confident at calculating musical intervals.

Instead of simply memorizing intervals, MatrixIntervals turns interval calculation into a focused and repeatable exercise built around musical relationships.
